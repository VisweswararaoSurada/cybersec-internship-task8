# VPN Setup & Testing Report

## VPN Chosen
- **VeePN(Free Tier)**

---

## Steps Taken
1. Installed VeePN extension in chrome broser.  
2. Logged in and connected to the nearest server.  
3. Verified new IP address via `whatismyipaddress`.  
4. Confirmed encrypted browsing on HTTPS sites.  
5. Disconnected and compared speeds.  

---

## Benefits of VPN
- Hides real IP address.  
- Encrypts traffic from ISP snooping.  
- Useful on public Wi-Fi.  
- Can bypass geo-restrictions.  

---

## Limitations
- Free plans have limited servers.  
- Slight reduction in speed due to encryption.  
- VPN does **not guarantee full anonymity** (logs, browser tracking still possible).  

---

## Conclusion
VPNs are a critical privacy tool.  
When combined with secure browsing practices, they greatly enhance online security and privacy.
